Picture a grand Roman festive procession winding through sunlit streets, capturing a vivid scene to tie these five words together:

1. **Agmen** (column, procession): A long, orderly **agmen** (procession) of citizens and soldiers marches through the city gates in celebration.
2. **Ago** (to drive, lead, do): At the front, a proud magistrate steps forward to **ago** (lead, drive) the ceremony, directing the flow of the parade.
3. **Alacer** (eager, brisk): Right beside him walks an **alacer** (eager, brisk) standard-bearer, carrying his banner with a lively, bouncing stride.
4. **Albus** (white): He leads a magnificent, snow-**albus** (white) bull adorned with festive garlands for the offering.
5. **Aliquis** (someone, anyone): Peering out from the cheering crowd on the sidelines, **aliquis** (someone) tosses a handful of fragrant flower petals onto the path.
