Imagine an artist standing in a tight stone alleyway looking up at a grand, sunlit cathedral square, bringing these five macronized Latin words together:

31. **amō** (to love): A passionate painter stands with his hand over his heart, completely **amō** (loving) the dramatic view before him.
32. **amplus** (large, spacious): Ahead opens up an **amplus** (large, spacious) piazza flooded with brilliant golden sunlight.
33. **an** (or, whether - introducing an alternative): He hesitates, wondering **an** (whether) he should set up his easel in the wide square or stay where he is.
34. **angustus** (narrow): Right behind him is the **angustus** (narrow) stone corridor he just walked through, casting deep, cool shadows.
35. **animus** (mind, spirit, courage): With a bold **animus** (spirit, courage), he lifts his heavy wooden box of paints and steps forward into the light to begin his masterpiece.
