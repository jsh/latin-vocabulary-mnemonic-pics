Picture a young Roman magistrate stepping into his new career, marching through the bustling city to tie these five words together:

1. **Adulescens** (young man): A bright-eyed **adulescens** (young man) stands on the marble steps of the senate, adjusting his pristine toga for his first day in public office.
2. **Adadipiscor / Adipiscor** (to obtain, win): He has come to **adipiscor** (obtain) his official civic honor and leadership role.
3. **Administro** (to manage, administer): Once inside the hall, he begins to **administro** (manage) his new duties, reviewing scrolls and organizing imperial decrees with sharp focus.
4. **Admodum** (very, quite): The workload is **admodum** (quite) heavy, piling high across his ornate wooden desk.
5. **Adventus** (arrival): Yet, the crowd outside grows restless and excited with the anticipated **adventus** (arrival) of the consul himself, marking a major turning point in the day's ceremonies.
