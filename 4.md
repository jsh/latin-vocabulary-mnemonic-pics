2. **Aetas** (age, period of life): An elderly farmer of advanced **aetas** (age) pauses from his heavy labor in the fields to wipe the sweat from his brow.
3. **Ager** (field): He stands right in the middle of a vast, parched **ager** (field) where crops are struggling against the drought.
4. **Aestimo** (to value, assess): Squinting at the withered stalks, he stops to **aestimo** (assess, value) the severe crop loss, calculating how much grain has survived.
5. **Agger** (mound, rampart): To protect the remaining produce from a dry wind, he looks over toward a sturdy earth **agger** (mound, rampart) he built along the property edge last season.
