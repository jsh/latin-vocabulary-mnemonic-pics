2. **Aedificium** (building): Behind him, a grand stone **aedificium** (building) acts
as a sturdy windbreak for the crowded stalls.
3. **Aeger** (sick, weary): Nearby, an **aeger** (sick, weary) traveler sits bundled up
on a bench, shivering and trying to stay out of the gale.
4. **Aequo** (to make level, equal): An apothecary next door works frantically to
**aequo** (level, make even) the precious healing powders on his hanging brass scales.

(bronze), which clinks softly as he weighs out coins and remedies to help the ailing
traveler.
