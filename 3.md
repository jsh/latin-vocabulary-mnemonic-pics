Imagine a bustling Roman marketplace whipped by a sudden dust storm, providing a vivid scene to tie these five words together:

1. **Adversus** (against, facing): A stoic merchant stands **adversus** (facing) the harsh, howling wind, leaning forward to keep his footing on the paving stones.
2. **Aedificium** (building): Behind him, a grand stone **aedificium** (building) acts as a sturdy windbreak for the crowded stalls.
3. **Aeger** (sick, weary): Nearby, an **aeger** (sick, weary) traveler sits bundled up on a bench, shivering and trying to stay out of the gale.
4. **Aequo** (to make level, equal): An apothecary next door works frantically to **aequo** (level, make even) the precious healing powders on his hanging brass scales.
5. **Aes** (bronze, copper, money): The swinging scale is made of gleaming **aes** (bronze), which clinks softly as he weighs out coins and remedies to help the ailing traveler.
